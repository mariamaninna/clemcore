It depends on your travel preferences, budget, and the length of your trip! Here’s a quick comparison to help you decide:

### ✈️ **Economy Class**
- **Best for:** Budget-conscious travelers, short trips, or those who don’t mind limited comfort.
- **Pros:**
- Most affordable option
- Widely available
- Good for travelers who don’t need extra space or perks
- **Cons:**
- Limited legroom and seat recline
- Basic meal service (often paid)
- No priority boarding or lounge access

### 💼 **Business Class**
- **Best for:** Travelers seeking comfort, convenience, and premium service—especially on long-haul flights.
- **Pros:**
- More spacious seating (often flat beds on long flights)
- Priority check-in, boarding, and baggage handling
- Premium meals and drinks
- Access to airport lounges
- Enhanced in-flight entertainment
- **Cons:**
- Significantly more expensive
- May not be worth it for short flights

### 🤔 How to Decide?
- **Choose Economy if:** You're traveling on a tight budget, for a short trip, or if you're a frequent flyer with airline status.
- **Choose Business if:** You're traveling long-haul, value comfort and time, or want a more relaxing experience.

👉 **Pro Tip:** If you're unsure, consider **premium economy**—it offers a middle ground with more legroom, better meals, and some lounge access at a lower cost than business class.

Let me know your destination, travel dates, and how long the flight is, and I can help you decide which class makes the most sense!
