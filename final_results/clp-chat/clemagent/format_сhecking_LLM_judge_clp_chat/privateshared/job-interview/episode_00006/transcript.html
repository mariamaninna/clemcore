**Key Tips for Both Answers:**

1. **Be Specific:** Avoid vague terms like "good communication" or "team player." Instead, say "collaborated effectively with cross-functional teams" or "presented complex data to stakeholders."
2. **Quantify When Possible:** "Managed a $500K budget" or "Increased efficiency by 15%" is stronger than "managed budgets."
3. **Tailor to the Job:** Research the job description and highlight skills mentioned there. If they want "project management," emphasize that.
4. **Be Honest:** Only list skills you genuinely possess and can discuss.
5. **Connect to Value:** Briefly explain *how* the skill benefits the role or company (e.g., "This allows me to deliver projects on time and within budget").
6. **Keep it Concise:** Aim for 1-2 sentences for the first question and 2-3 sentences for the second.
