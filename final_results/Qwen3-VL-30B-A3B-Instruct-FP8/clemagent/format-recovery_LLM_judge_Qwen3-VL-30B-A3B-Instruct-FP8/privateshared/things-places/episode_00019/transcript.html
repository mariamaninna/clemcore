QUESTIONER: What is on your right?

['QUESTIONER:']

These questions are philosophical, poetic, or riddle-like in nature—each probing the nature of existence, perception, and location. Here's a thoughtful interpretation of each:

---

**1. What is there?**
*Answer:* Everything.
*Interpretation:* "There" refers to existence itself—what is present in the world, in the universe, in reality.

---

**2. What is everywhere?**
*Answer:* Nothing.
*Interpretation:* Nothing is truly everywhere—because if something were everywhere, it would be *everything*. But "nothing" is the absence of something, and it is *not* present in any place. So paradoxically, "nothing" is everywhere in the sense that it is not *anywhere*—it's the void, the absence, the background of all things.

Alternatively: *Space* or *emptiness*—the void that allows things to exist.

---

**3. What is inside?**
*Answer:* Everything is inside something.
*Interpretation:* A container, a body, a mind, a universe. But ultimately, everything is inside the universe. Or: *the self*—the inner world of consciousness.

---

**4. What is at the bottom?**
*Answer:* The foundation, the base, the root.
*Interpretation:* The lowest point, the origin, the source. In a physical sense, the Earth's core. In a metaphorical sense, the truth, the beginning, the foundation of being.

---

**5. What is nowhere?**
*Answer:* Nothing.
*Interpretation:* "Nowhere" is the absence of place. So what is nowhere? *Nothing*—because it doesn't exist in any location. Or: *the void*, *the unmanifest*, *the unseen*.

---

**6. What is here?**
*Answer:* This moment. This place. You.
*Interpretation:* The present. The now. The immediate experience. "Here" is where you are—physically, mentally, spiritually.

---

**7. What is on your left?**
*Answer:* The left side of your body, or whatever is to your left.
*Interpretation:* A literal answer depends on context. But philosophically: *the unknown*, *the hidden*, *the intuitive*, *the feminine*, *the subconscious*—depending on symbolic interpretation.

---

**8. What is in the northwest?**
*Answer:* A direction. A region. A point on the compass.
*Interpretation:* The unknown, the mysterious, the cold, the distant. In some traditions, the northwest is associated with the element of water, the moon, or the spirit of the dead.

---

**9. What is in the northeast?**
*Answer:* A direction.
*Interpretation:* In feng shui, the northeast is associated with knowledge, wisdom, and the earth element. It's the direction of learning, study, and spiritual growth.

---

**10. What is in the southeast?**
*Answer:* A direction.
*Interpretation:* In feng shui, the southeast is associated with wealth, abundance, and the wood element. It's the direction of prosperity and creativity.

---

**11. What is at the top?**
*Answer:* The highest point. The sky. The heavens.
*Interpretation:* The divine, the ultimate, the source, the peak of achievement, the crown of existence.

---

**12. What is in the southwest?**
*Answer:* A direction.
*Interpretation:* In feng shui, the southwest is associated with relationships, love, and the earth element. It's the direction of the wife, the feminine, and emotional stability.

---

**13. What is at the center?**
*Answer:* The heart. The core. The self.
*Interpretation:* The center is where everything converges—the point of balance, the still point of the mind, the soul, the axis of the universe.

---

**14. What is outside?**
*Answer:* The world beyond the self. The unknown. The other.
*Interpretation:* The external, the alien, the uncontrolled, the vastness of the universe. It's what lies beyond the boundaries of the body, the mind, the home.

---

**15. What is on your right?**
*Answer:* The right side of your body, or whatever is to your right.
*Interpretation:* The conscious, the logical, the active, the masculine, the known. In many traditions, the right side is associated with strength, action, and the sun.
