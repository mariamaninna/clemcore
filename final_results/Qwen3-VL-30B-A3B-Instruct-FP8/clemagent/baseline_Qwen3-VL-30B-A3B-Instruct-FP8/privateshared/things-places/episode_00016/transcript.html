### 2. **Philosophical Interpretation**
- **Nowhere** can represent the unknown, the unattainable, or the undefined.
- Think of it as the opposite of "everywhere" — a state of non-existence or non-location.

- "Nowhere" can also be a metaphor for **infinity** or **limitlessness** — the idea that something is not confined to any specific point.

---

---
