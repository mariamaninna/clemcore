**e ≈ 2.71828**

### Key facts about **e**:
- It is an **irrational number**, meaning it cannot be expressed as a simple fraction.
- It is also **transcendental**, meaning it is not a root of any non-zero polynomial equation with rational coefficients.
- It arises naturally in many areas, including:
- **Compound interest** (continuous compounding)

- **Exponential growth and decay**
- **Probability and statistics** (e.g., the normal distribution)

### Definition:
One common definition of **e** is the limit:

$$
e = \lim_{n \to \infty} \left(1 + \frac{1}{n}\right)^n
$$

Another is the sum of the infinite series:

$$
e = \sum_{n=0}^{\infty} \frac{1}{n!} = 1 + \frac{1}{1!} + \frac{1}{2!} + \frac{1}{3!} + \cdots
$$

So, in short:
**e ≈ 2.71828** — a cornerstone of mathematics.
